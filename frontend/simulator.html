How to use it

  1. On the Phone tab, type the USSD code or tap the shortcut under the call button, then press the green call button.
  2. A menu pops up. Type the number of an option (for example 1) and press Send. Keep going until the menu ends, or press Cancel.
  3. Choose the option to get the plan by SMS and pay with Mobile Money. A PIN prompt appears on the phone: type 0000 and press Approve to pay, or press Reject to see what happens when payment fails.
  4. Tap the Messages tab (the envelope icon) to read the texts that arrived. A red number on the icon means new messages.
  5. In Messages you can also text ARAPAI FARM TWIN yourself: HELP, PLAN, LANG SW.
