ARAPAI FARM TWIN · smartphone version

The same app a farmer uses on a smartphone: it installs from the browser, downloads the farm’s NASA data once, and then keeps working without internet.

This is the live app, shown at smartphone width. You can scroll it, move the sliders and save plans.

You are on a phone already

Open the app

Open it on your own smartphone

Point your phone camera at the code, or type this address:

farm-twin-5n9n.onrender.com

The first load can take up to a minute if the free server has been asleep.

Install it like an app

  • Android (Chrome): open the menu (⋮) and choose Install app or Add to Home screen.
  • iPhone (Safari): tap Share, then Add to Home Screen.

Try it with no internet (30 seconds)

  1. Open the app once while you are online. This downloads the farm’s NASA data. Wait for the line “NASA data up to …”.
  2. Turn on airplane mode.
  3. Open the app again. The header says Offline, and the ranking is still there.
  4. Move a weight slider, change last season’s crop, or press Save. Everything works on the phone. A saved plan waits for internet and is sent when you are back online.

No smartphone?

Farmers with a basic phone get the same advice by USSD and SMS. Try the phone simulator and dial *384*2026#.

SMS and mobile money are simulated in this prototype.

Without internet the app uses the data it already downloaded and shows its date; new NASA data needs a connection. We tested offline mode in desktop Chrome. Installing and offline use on real phones is the next thing we will test.
